# ASH//SIGNAL: FRACTURED REALMS — v0.3 roadmap

Five selectable authored worlds replace the single campaign: Soulforge, Tide, Pyre, Neon and Veil. Each is built for more than five minutes of live gameplay, with distinct traversal, story, machinery and boss variants. Current legal-run timing, deep-harness results, APK inspection and CI status belong in LEVEL.md and VALIDATION.md. Human addictiveness, balance and Android comfort remain playtesting questions.

## Completed sequence

1. **Controls:**112px analogue stick and four curved actions in a reserved112px bottom gutter. Tap Attack slashes/hold180ms fires; tap Jump leaps/hold350ms thrusts; tap Power casts/contextually interacts/hold450ms opens paused Sorcery/gear; Dash remains direct. A44px center pause button, independent pointers and an adaptive full-width game viewport keep controls outside play. Retain keyboard/gamepad verbs and focus pause.
2. **Physical villains:** moving ground enemies and advancing/retreating/leaping bosses; shared core/hand/launcher origins, directional endpoint beams, visible rockets, portal exit warnings, recovery and shield/weakened/enraged states.
3. **Action presentation:**347 original textures, including a distinct pointed-hat/violet-eyed Ysra portrait, one-pixel character detail, stable anchors, overhead/horizontal/finisher/lunge poses, wizard/witch/survivor expressions, shield/pickup animation and mechanical traps. Remove stage cones; use foreground rims and compact world decor with reused full backgrounds.
4. **Combat:** three swords, five guns, three armors, nine spells, finite cannon/nova charges, light/heavy attack distinction, Borrowed Spark parry resonance and Break Ward suppression. Bounded clone/wolf/dragon allies telegraph arrival, attack and expire.
5. **Realms and saves:** sea/current/raft routes, volcanic steam/crumble/conduit routes, techno lifts/shutters and wizard mirror passages. Share discoveries while retaining separate realm relays/claims/bosses/gates/props. Local retries preserve valid resources; reward choices may carry disclosed consequences.
6. **Delivery:** unit/world proofs, browser controls/UI/replay, asset exports, configured30-minute wall-clock deep harness, production build and offline Android debug APK. Version3/minAPI24, Vite6.4.4, CI artifact ASH-SIGNAL-Fractured-Realms-debug-APK. Report actual results separately from hardware evaluation.

Atlas specialists owned disjoint controls, art/audio, combat/physics, worlds and research; the lead integrated rendering, packaging and QA. Plain-JavaScript simulation remains Node-testable; DOM UI reads state through callbacks and Phaser renders it. Android hosts bundled assets without another physics system or Internet permission.

References inform mechanics and pacing; artwork, names and code remain original. No Unity/Unreal/Blender or paid-generation dependency is required. Physical controller calibration, speaker mix, human challenge and sustained Android performance still need device playtesting. Production signing/store distribution are separate from this debug delivery.

Field guide

Touch · two ways to act

Drag an empty part of the screen to move. Hold Attack and drag on its button to aim independently. The stick stays where your finger first lands.

Attack
Tap to slash. Hold to fire.
Jump
Tap to leap. Hold to thrust.
Boost
Tap to propel forward, even in the air. Hold to thrust upward. Both use fuel.
Power rune
Tap to cast, or interact when someone or something is nearby. Hold to open equipment.

The small gear opens equipment while the world is paused. Hold down + Jump to drop through a thin platform.

Movement & survival

Jump again in the air. Hold a jump for more height; jump against a wall to climb. Thrust uses fuel, which refills on solid ground.

Read an enemy's windup, evade the strike, then punish its recovery. Slash incoming projectiles to deflect them. Find caches and speak to survivors for equipment.

Sorcery consumes resonance. Cannon and nova charges are finite. Collect the three runes to open the sanctuary's heart.

Portals & the Soul Engine

Walk into an active portal to travel. Step clear before entering again.

Gather souls released by fallen guardians. Open Gear → Soul Engine to forge armor and strengthen your lantern. Your forged upgrades remain after a fall.

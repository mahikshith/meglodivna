Gamepad: stick move/aim · A jump · X slash · RT fire · B dash · RB thrust · LT magic · Y interact · LB gun · D-pad left/right sword/spell · right stick click nova.

Touch anywhere clear, then drag to move and aim. The stick stays at your first touch and fades eight seconds after release. The three large buttons are Dash, Attack, and Jump. Attack cuts immediately; hold it to fire and its icon becomes a gun. Jump leaps immediately; hold it to thrust and its icon becomes a jetpack. The separate power rune casts your selected spell or interacts with someone nearby. Open the small gear button to choose equipment and companions while paused. Holding the rune also opens gear. Down + jump drops through catwalks.

Fire spreads. Shadow hides. Lightning staggers foes. Sorcery consumes soul energy; cannon and nova charges are finite. Explore caches and speak to survivors for gear.

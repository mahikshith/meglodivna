# Story and visual correction pass

The v0.9 test build passed mechanical simulations but missed the player's visual and narrative acceptance criteria. Checkmarks in the October v0.9 checklist record that historical delivery; they do not approve the artwork or flow for this correction. This pass treats the following as release blockers, in order:

1. **Readability at phone size.** Remove forced pixel sampling from the game canvas and UI illustrations. Inspect each live world at 740-by-360 and 960-by-540. Replace or re-extract any low-resolution or blurred background, terrain, prop, portal, enemy or companion that still reads as pixel art. The foreground route and actors must remain clear against each backdrop.
2. **One story route.** Present one active objective and one reachable crossing at a time: Windward Canopy, Moonlit Causeway, then Ember Oath. Replace the three adjacent hub choices and repeated unexplained levers with visible causes and effects. Give each crossing a distinct action puzzle with a safe introduction, a practiced variation, and a final combination under pressure. Keep legacy gate and save identifiers so existing progress survives.
3. **Boost feel.** Recover the v0.8 sustained flight response. A tap may produce the requested airborne forward burst; holding Boost must transition into clean upward thrust without a compulsory horizontal lurch or burst fuel charge. Recheck firing, jumping, crouching and touch input together.
4. **Character and effect quality.** Review the Minotaur, full-body dragon, wolf, all companions and each live secondary villain at actual display size. Preserve anatomy, moving poses, weapon sockets and collision geometry while upgrading weak source art. Give the crossing portal a clear, illustrated activation sequence.
5. **Playable chapter proof.** Check the opening and closing story beats, objective text, route order, failure recovery and final boss through ordinary controls. Retain visual fixtures separately from complete legal runs and physical Android testing.

Acceptance is based on the compiled game and phone-sized captures, not source sheets alone. A new Android delivery needs a fresh signed APK, all required checks, an uploaded-byte comparison, and an updated README link to that exact APK. Automated route timings cannot establish first-time human enjoyment or physical-device comfort.

The level approach draws on [Matt Thorson's Celeste workshop](https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1024307/Level-Design-Workshop-Designing-Celeste), which links area story to platforming, [Valve's Portal 2 chamber design account](https://gameinformer.com/b/features/archive/2010/03/17/thinking-with-portals-making-a-test-chamber), which teaches one rule before turning it into a combined challenge, [Moon Studios' Ori design discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/pcgaming/comments/hfpsj8/we_are_moon_studios_creators_of_the_ori_franchise/), which treats a finale as a fair test of learned skills, and [Team Cherry's Hollow Knight interview](https://www.nintendo.com/au/news-and-articles/the-metamorphosis-of-hollow-knight-with-team-cherry-aussie-developer-interview/), which ties exploration to a coherent world. In this build, each Wind, Water and Fire crossing introduces its force in a teaching court, changes its direction or timing through four distinct seven-landing sections, requires a visible high-lantern landing to open each shutter, and ends with a guardian. Low catch ledges, permanent relays, and the forge maintenance walk give room to recover. Side reliquaries reward exploration without hiding the main route. These are design references, not copied levels or assets.

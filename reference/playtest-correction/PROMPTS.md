# Built-in image generation prompts

The text below records the production prompts used for these source images. Input paths are repository-relative; every resulting source PNG is retained in this directory.

## sanctuary-source.png

Inputs: `public/lantern/sanctuary.png`, `reference/epic-revision/realms-raw.png`.

> Create one original production-ready PANORAMIC BACKGROUND for a 2D side-scrolling illustrated game. This is the main Lantern sanctuary at the start of a Ramayana-inspired original fantasy journey, not a literal depiction of a sacred person. Landscape 16:9 composition, high-resolution, smooth fine painterly rendering with crisp clean silhouettes, no pixel art, no mosaic, no blur, no fake compression, no UI/text. Approved art direction: moonlit deep blue-green ruined stone, old carved arches, natural roots and moss, restrained ivory moon edge light, very few small amber lanterns. Camera is level side view at human height. Compose three depth bands: distant broken aqueduct and sky (upper half, atmospheric but crisp); middle ruins and one large tree silhouette (center); a deliberately QUIET dark teal lower third behind the playable character and platforms, no painted walkable platforms or foreground collision cues. Distinct broad shapes and open negative space around the horizontal gameplay band. Architectural influence from ancient Indian stone stepwells and lotus carving, original fictional setting, no European Gothic spires. It must retain enough contrast for a 50px tall ivory/teal hero and bronze armored Minotaur in front. Seam-compatible left and right edges in color/value without hard vertical discontinuities. Use the supplied prior art only for Lantern palette and material finish, redesign composition for gameplay clarity.
